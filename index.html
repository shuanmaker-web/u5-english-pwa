閱讀測驗 (Peculiar Museums)

When you think of museums, what comes to mind? You might think of paintings, sculptures, and antique artifacts. You probably won't immediately think of alien spacecraft, socks, and carrots. Yet there are museums for all of those things. In fact, mention any object, and it probably has its own museum.

當你想到博物館時，腦中會浮現什麼？你可能會想到畫作、雕塑和古董文物。你大概不會馬上想到外星太空船、襪子和紅蘿蔔。然而，這些東西全都有博物館。事實上，隨便說出一樣物品，大概都有它自己的博物館。

Take ramen noodles, for example. A museum in Yokohama, Japan, tells the story of this popular food. You can see bowls, chopsticks, and some of the machinery that the Japanese used to make ramen. There is even a floor in the museum that looks like Tokyo in 1958. If all of this whets your appetite, you'll most likely want to buy a bowl of ramen noodles before you leave.

以拉麵為例。日本橫濱有一間博物館，講述這種受歡迎食物的故事。你可以看到碗、筷子，以及日本人過去用來製作拉麵的一些機器。博物館裡甚至有一層樓看起來就像 1958 年的東京。如果這一切勾起了你的食慾，你離開前很可能會想買一碗拉麵來吃。

Meanwhile, in other parts of the world, you can find many more peculiar museums. In the United States, for example, you can visit the Museum of Bad Art. This museum assembles, preserves, and displays the worst art imaginable. It has paintings in which people look like monsters and mountains look like ice cream cones. Another American town is home to the Salt and Pepper Shaker Museum. This museum is the result of one woman's obsession with the small tabletop items. The museum currently exhibits 22,000 sets in shapes as varied as McDonald's menu items, skeletons, and every conceivable vegetable. Visitors are welcome in the gift shop if they are keen to start their own collections! Over in Europe, the Museum of Torture is popular with tourists. It has more than 60 torture instruments inside. They were all intended to hurt people in very dreadful ways.

同時，在世界其他地方，你還能找到更多奇特的博物館。例如在美國，你可以參觀「爛藝術博物館」（Museum of Bad Art）。這間博物館收集、保存並展示你所能想像最糟糕的藝術品。館內的畫作裡，人看起來像怪物，山看起來像冰淇淋甜筒。美國另一個小鎮則有「鹽罐與胡椒罐博物館」。這間博物館源自一位女士對這些小巧桌上用品的癡迷。館內目前展出 22,000 組鹽罐胡椒罐，形狀五花八門，有麥當勞菜單上的品項、骷髏，以及各種你想得到的蔬菜。如果你熱衷於開始自己的收藏，歡迎到禮品店逛逛！在歐洲，「酷刑博物館」很受觀光客歡迎。館內有超過 60 件刑具，全都是為了以非常可怕的方式傷害人而製作的。

So the next time you want some exposure to culture, forget about New York's Museum of Modern Art and Michelangelo's David. Instead, check out the Lawnmower Museum, the Dog Collar Museum, or the International Museum of Toilets. You're sure to have a lot of fun!

所以下次你想接觸文化時，就別去紐約現代藝術博物館或米開朗基羅的《大衛》了。相反地，去看看割草機博物館、狗項圈博物館，或國際馬桶博物館吧。你一定會玩得很開心！

混合題 (Working Holidays)

Vacations can be very expensive. You have to pay for plane tickets, hotels, and food. But never fear, there is an answer: a working holiday. On a working holiday, you can travel and work at the same time, therefore solving the problem of cost. Take a look at the following four people sharing their working holiday experiences.

度假可能非常昂貴。你得支付機票、旅館和餐飲費用。但別擔心，有一個解決辦法：打工度假。打工度假時，你可以邊旅行邊工作，因此解決了費用的問題。來看看以下四個人分享他們的打工度假經驗。

(A) Chris

I spent the last summer working as a balloon chaser on my working holiday in Europe. Assisting the hot-air balloon crew, I used maps and information from the pilot to figure out the perfect landing spot. They were very patient and friendly. Some of the people I worked with during that time even became my very close friends.

去年夏天，我在歐洲打工度假，擔任熱氣球追逐員。我協助熱氣球的工作團隊，利用地圖和駕駛員提供的資訊，找出完美的降落地點。他們非常有耐心又友善。我在那段期間共事的一些人，甚至成為我非常親近的朋友。

(B) Andrea

My deep passion for coffee led me to choose to work in a café on my working holiday. At first, I had a hard time remembering all the terms and recipes for different coffee creations in a foreign language. However, with practice, I overcame the difficulty and gained confidence in my barista skills.

我對咖啡的深厚熱情，讓我選擇在打工度假時到咖啡廳工作。一開始，我很難用外語記住各種咖啡特調的專有名詞和配方。不過，經過練習，我克服了困難，也對自己的咖啡師技能有了信心。

(C) Pamela

Spending my working holiday in the French Alps as a hotel staff member was a dream come true. I got to embrace the beauty of the mountains while working in the hotel industry. When I wasn't assisting guests, I went skiing or just chilled out and enjoyed the landscape. It was a truly unforgettable experience.

在法國阿爾卑斯山擔任飯店員工度過我的打工度假，簡直是美夢成真。我得以在飯店業工作的同時，擁抱群山之美。不需要協助客人的時候，我就去滑雪，或只是放鬆欣賞風景。那是一段真正難忘的經歷。

(D) Leonard

I worked as a koala catcher during my working holiday in Australia. We needed to protect the wild koalas by capturing them so that the government could release them into safer areas. This might look fun, but it's also an important job—if the koalas are not caught, they most likely die.

我在澳洲打工度假期間擔任無尾熊捕捉員。我們需要捕捉野生無尾熊來保護牠們，好讓政府能把牠們野放到更安全的地區。這看起來也許很好玩，但這也是一份重要的工作：如果沒有捕捉到無尾熊，牠們很可能會死亡。
